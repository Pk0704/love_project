The Obstacle Thesis

From Plato's ladder to the Tinder feed, love in Western art is a history of mediation.

Every era invents a new obstacle for love to pass through — the chapel, the troubadour's distance, the novel, the veil, the screen.

Love without an obstacle isn't recognisable as love.

We are in love with the obstacle.
